## Bug Squash Interview [Functional]

### What to expect & how to prepare

In this 45 minute interview, you will meet with a Stripe Software Engineer who will look to understand how you understand and navigate an unfamiliar, but well-written, idiomatic code base in your chosen programming language. You’ll navigate a large codebase to spot and fix bugs in the code. Tip: While prepping, focus on your strategy and approach to debugging rather than aimlessly looking for bugs in code.

### What we’re evaluating

In this interview we will be looking to get a sense for the following:

- Forward progress
- Tooling
- Understanding of the code
- Interaction and Collaboration
